how I work

From a working agent to a defensible decision.

The question I carry from engineering into safety research: what evidence would change our decision to deploy?

  1. 01

    Build within boundaries

    Specify the task, permissions, budget, and human owner before delegating work.

  2. 02

    Test the failure modes

    Evaluate performance alongside misuse, oversight evasion, and recovery.

  3. 03

    Challenge the evidence

    Check what the evaluator misses, who can alter the record, and whether results generalise.

  4. 04

    Make the decision explicit

    Connect claims to evidence, record uncertainty, and define when to stop or reassess.
