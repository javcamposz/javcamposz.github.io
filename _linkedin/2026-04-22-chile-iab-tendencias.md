Last Sunday I landed back at Heathrow after an incredibly intensive,
dawn-to-dusk week in Santiago, Chile. If there is one thing I took away from
this trip, it is that the Latin American market is not just preparing for the
AI revolution — they are already building it.

The operating rhythm was relentless. My days started at 5:00 AM syncing with
our London and Tilburg teams, squeezing beautiful morning runs across Santiago
adding to 35 km, and then running back-to-back meetings until 7:30 PM.

A massive thank you to the stellar Cape.io (formerly Peach) Chile team —
Henry Northcote, Ismael Larrain Mac Clure, Maria Corina Curti — for
orchestrating such a flawless, high-density week.

Highlights:

- Strategic deep-dives with major brands and agencies.
- An incredible debate organised by Cámara de Empresas Creativas and
  the great Raul Menijibar.
- A fantastic video recording and interview session with Rodrigo Saavedra
  from IAB Chile.
- Speaking at the packed IAB *Tendencias 2026* event, discussing how AI must
  move from a novelty tool to a transversal layer of corporate trust. Awesome
  debate moderated by Carolina Garlick Portnoy.

Across all those C-level conversations, one profound question kept surfacing —
the oldest dilemma in advertising, famously coined by John Wanamaker:

> *"I know I waste half of my marketing budget, but I do not know which half."*

Can AI finally solve this?

From a first-principles perspective, the historical problem has always been
the broken bridge between **action** (short-term sales / conversion) and
**impact** (long-term brand building). The data has always existed in silos.

The answer is yes — AI can solve this, but not with the passive LLMs we use
today, nor with the broken datasets. It requires **agentic AI**.

When you deploy an autonomous system that governs the entire campaign
lifecycle, the silo collapses. An agentic framework can generate the creative,
deploy it across platforms, instantly ingest the media performance data, and
recursively learn exactly which creative variable drove the sale versus which
one built the brand memory. It closes the attribution loop in real-time,
optimising your budget autonomously — and it keeps doing it over time, without
forgetting.

The technology to finally know which half of your budget is working is
already here.

I explore the exact architecture needed to deploy these autonomous, ROI-driven
systems in *Autonomous Minds*.

Gracias por todo, Chile. 🇨🇱
