Everyone mocked Apple for losing the AI race. It may be the only company whose
AI position survives the next two years.

From a first-principles perspective, three things stand out: no frontier model
moat has survived for long, unified memory changes the economics of local AI,
and open models are narrowing the gap to the frontier.

The runtime for AI agents is moving onto the desk and into the pocket, and
Apple already owns that ground.

If your organisation's AI strategy assumes agents live in someone else's cloud
forever, you are building on the layer with the shortest half-life. Stop
chasing the frontier. Start owning the runtime.
