I gave Claude Fable 5 a full week as my daily driver. By Friday I was pricing
the switch to OpenAI, and the refusal that pushed me there had nothing to do
with safety.

From a first-principles perspective, alignment is a control mechanism, a
steering wheel. I got a locked door.

I asked it to help build evals for recursive self-improvement models: measuring
future model performance, nowhere near cyber or bio. It refused. Another model
did the same task that day.

A guardrail that refuses the person building the safety evals reroutes the work
to whichever vendor says yes. The risk survives; the revenue moves.
