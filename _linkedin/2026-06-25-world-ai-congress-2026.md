The era of "pick the best model and win" is officially over. I watched a room
of global AI leaders bury it in London.

At AI World Congress, every hallway conversation circled the same conclusion:
the model was never the moat. The advantage sits in what you build around it.

Everyone rents the same frontier models. The edge moves to proprietary data,
agentic systems wired into core processes, and governance built in from day one.

The old moat was choosing the best model. The new moat is owning the loop it
learns on.
