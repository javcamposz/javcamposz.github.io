I told my engineering team to spawn parallel subagents. I left out the cost,
which was printed in the same document.

The document was Anthropic's post on building multi-agent systems. I carried
across the exciting line: parallel agents can cover more ground than a single
agent working within its context limits. I did not carry across the sentence
saying that, in their own testing, multi-agent systems typically use 3 to 10
times more tokens than single-agent approaches for equivalent tasks.

My engineers paid for that. They built to my direction, carrying a cost the
document named and my summary did not.

Now I forward the constraint before the capability. If I cannot repeat a
document's caveat from memory, I have not read it. I have shopped it for
permission.
